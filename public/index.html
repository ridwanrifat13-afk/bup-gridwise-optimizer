Test Benchmark & Operator Directives Guide

How Operator Notes Translate to Dispatch Rules

The LLM reads unconstrained operator notes, classifies them into strict typed adjustments with mathematical guardrails, and passes them to the linear program solver.

Directive 1: Solar Reduction
Cloud & Panel Degradation
"Solar output will drop to about 20% from 1 PM to 3 PM."
Hours [13, 14] solar scaled by factor 0.20
Directive 2: Inhibit Window
Battery Safety Lockout
"Do not charge the battery between 2 PM and 4 PM."
Hours [14, 15] charge_rate enforced to 0 kW
Directive 3: Grid Import Cap
Peak Demand Shaving
"Cap grid imports at 155 kWh from 6 PM to 9 PM."
Hours [18, 19, 20] grid_kwh ≤ 155 kWh
Directive 4: Distractor Filter
Irrelevant Operational Notes
"The cafeteria menu changes tomorrow."
Directive type classified as no_op (ignored)
